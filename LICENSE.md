This repository has no single licence. Each audio file keeps the licence of the recording it was made from, as listed
in [ATTRIBUTION.md](ATTRIBUTION.md) and in the `license` and `licenseUrl` fields of `manifest.json`.

|---|---|
| `azan/london/*` | CC0 1.0 Universal: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ |
| `azan/nabawi/*`, `azan/malmo/*` | Creative Commons Attribution 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ |
| `azan/shalkar/*`, `azan/aaqib/*`, `azan/ghana/*` | Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/ |

- **CC BY.** Reuse must credit the author, name the licence, link to the source and say that the file was edited, as
  ATTRIBUTION.md does.
- **CC BY-SA.** The edited files here are adaptations, so they stay under CC BY-SA 4.0. Anything made from them must be
  shared under the same licence.
- **CC0.** No conditions; credit is given anyway.
- **No technical restrictions.** The files are published here as plain downloads, without DRM, so anyone can use them
  under their licences.

The text files (`README.md`, `ATTRIBUTION.md`, `LICENSE.md`, `manifest.json`) are dedicated to the public domain under
CC0 1.0.

The recordings are provided as they are, without warranty. The licences cover the copyright in each recording as
granted by its uploader. They do not grant trademark, publicity or other rights.
