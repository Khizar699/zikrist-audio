# Attribution

Every audio file in this repository is an edited copy of the recording named below. "Edited" means, for every file:
leading and trailing silence trimmed, mixed down to mono, loudness-normalised (EBU R128, −16 LUFS) and re-encoded;
the `.caf` clips are also cut to the opening phrases with a 0.6 s fade-out and resampled to 22.05 kHz. Nothing else was
added, removed or rearranged inside the azan.
